CLEARPATH MECA PRIVACY POLICY

1. Who We Are

ClearPath Meca ("Meca") is made and sold by ClearPath Innovative Solutions LLC, doing business as
ClearPath AI ("we", "us"). You can reach us through the contact form at
https://nightfallcity.cpengine.store/contact.

Effective on the date ClearPath Meca 1.0.0 is first released.

This policy explains what Meca does with data on your PC, what leaves your PC and when, and what we
receive when you buy a key.

2. In Short

Camera images are processed on your PC and never uploaded. Hand tracking uses Google MediaPipe,
which sends Google usage and performance metrics (not your images); Meca can't turn this off. After
you consent on first run, Meca also asks GitHub for signed updates. Activation keys are checked
offline, on your PC.

3. Camera Images

Meca reads frames from the camera you choose and processes them in memory to find the positions of
your hand landmarks (points such as fingertips and knuckles). Each frame is then discarded. Frames
are not saved and not uploaded. This edition of Meca has no recording feature. Meca does not build
or keep a template of your hand.

4. Google MediaPipe Usage Metrics

Meca's hand tracking uses Google MediaPipe. The MediaPipe library sends Google usage and
performance metrics, for example library and operating system details and timing information, to
play.googleapis.com. These metrics do not include your camera images. Meca cannot turn this off.

Because of this, Meca asks for your consent on first run, before it starts hand tracking. If you
decline, Meca quits. Google handles these metrics under its own privacy policy:
https://policies.google.com/privacy.

5. Update Checks

After you consent, Meca asks GitHub for its signed update manifest when it starts, about once a
day while it runs, and when you choose Check for updates. When you install an update, Meca
downloads the new installer from the same place. Both requests go to github.com, which serves the files from GitHub's download servers
on githubusercontent.com (currently release-assets.githubusercontent.com). Like any download, this
lets GitHub see your IP address and Meca's version, which Meca sends as its User-Agent
(ClearPathMeca/<version>, for example ClearPathMeca/1.0.0). Meca makes no update check before you
consent. GitHub handles this under its own privacy statement:
https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies.

6. Activation Keys

Activation keys are checked offline, on your PC. The file license.txt in Meca's data folder holds
your key, and the key contains the email address you bought it with. When we refund a purchase, we
revoke its key through the signed update manifest. That manifest lists only fingerprints of revoked
keys, never the keys themselves or email addresses.

7. Data on Your PC

Meca keeps its data in the folder %LOCALAPPDATA%\ClearPath\Meca:
- meca.toml: your settings;
- license.txt: your activation key, if you entered one;
- state.json: your consent, the trial dates, the update check state and whether the Meca guide
  has been shown;
- status.json: while Meca runs, its format version, whether Meca is paused, locked (and why) or
  showing its preview, whether it is recording (only developer copies run from source can record),
  and its process number, which the Meca guide reads; Meca removes it when it quits, or when it
  next starts if it was closed some other way;
- logs\: Meca's log files (see section 8);
- updates\: the most recent downloaded update installer; older ones are deleted when a newer one
  is downloaded.

The registry key HKCU\Software\ClearPath\Meca holds FirstRun and LastSeen, the start of your trial
and the last time Meca saw the clock. They are kept after you uninstall Meca, so that reinstalling
does not restart the trial. The same key holds InstallDir, Version, DesktopShortcut and Autostart,
which the installer writes.

To delete everything: uninstall Meca with "Also delete my Meca settings, licence and logs" ticked,
then run this command in a Command Prompt:

    reg delete "HKCU\Software\ClearPath\Meca" /f

8. Logs

Meca's logs are kept on your PC only, and they are rotated so they stay at a few megabytes. Meca
sends nothing from them automatically. If you ask us for help, you choose whether to attach a log
to your support request.

9. Purchases

Keys are sold through our store at https://nightfallcity.cpengine.store (the "Store"). Payments are
handled by the Store's payment processor, Stripe, which collects your payment details, billing
address and tax information under its own privacy policy (https://stripe.com/privacy). We never see
your card details. From the Store we receive your name, email address, country, what you bought,
the price, tax and the order or refund status.

We use that information to create and email your activation key, to restore it if you lose it, to
answer support requests, to handle refunds and chargebacks (a refunded key is revoked, see section
6) and to keep the records the law requires. The Store keeps your email address, your order and
your key for these reasons. Your activation key contains the email address you bought with, your
plan and its dates, so anyone you share the key with can read them; please keep it private.

10. Children

Meca is not directed to children under 13. The end-user licence agreement requires you to be 18 or
older, or to have a parent or guardian accept it for you.

11. Your Choices and Rights

You can ask to access or delete the personal information we hold about you through the contact
form at https://nightfallcity.cpengine.store/contact. The data on your PC is under your control: you
can delete it at any time (section 7).

12. Changes

If this policy changes in a material way, Meca shows the change on the first run of the update
that brings it and asks for your consent again.
